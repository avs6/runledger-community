**RunLedger is an open-source AI operations control plane.** It helps teams understand what their AI systems are doing, what they cost, whether they are allowed, whether they worked, and what to optimize next.

RunLedger supports both:

- an **inline gateway** for routing, caching, budgets, and runtime controls
- an **out-of-band control plane** for SDK, OTLP, MCP, and webhook-driven telemetry

> Tracing tools tell you what happened.  
> RunLedger tells you what it cost, who owns it, whether it was allowed, whether it worked, and how to improve it.

## The problems it solves

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Spend explodes" icon="fire">
    Retry loops, oversized prompts, and weak routing quietly burn budget.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Attribution is weak" icon="circle-question">
    Cost is hard to tie back to tenant, user, feature, workflow, or model choice.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Routing ignores economics" icon="route">
    Model choice often optimizes for capability only, not cost-per-outcome.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No shared audit trail" icon="file-invoice">
    Observability, governance, and finance data usually live in separate systems.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

RunLedger closes all of these in one self-hosted system with no vendor lock-in and support for both hosted and local models.

See the [Community vs Enterprise](https://github.com/avs6/runledger-community#community-vs-enterprise) comparison.
