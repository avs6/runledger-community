The **Organizations** page is platform-admin-only. It is the lifecycle hub for every tenant on a RunLedger installation.

## What Platform Admins Can Do

- List every organization across the platform.
- Create a new organization with its own seeded org admin.
- View workspace and member counts.
- Edit organization name, plan, and lifecycle status.
- Suspend or reactivate non-default, non-current organizations.
- Delete non-default, non-current organizations with typed-name confirmation.

## Safety Rules

The default organization and the organization you are currently switched into cannot be suspended or deleted. Switch to another organization before deleting a non-default org.

Deleting an organization removes its workspaces and tenant memberships. Users who no longer belong to any organization or workspace are deactivated, while platform admins are preserved.

## Related Pages

- **Org Profile** is for org-admin self-service rename/profile edits inside one organization.
- **Users** manages user identity and membership.
- **Workspace** manages teams/workspaces inside the active organization.
