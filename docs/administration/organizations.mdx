The **Organizations** page is platform-admin-only. It is the canonical lifecycle hub for every tenant on a RunLedger installation.

## What Platform Admins Can Do

- List every organization across the platform.
- Create a new organization with its own seeded org admin.
- View workspace and member counts.
- Edit organization name, plan, and lifecycle status.
- Suspend or reactivate non-default, non-current organizations.
- Delete non-default, non-current organizations with typed-name confirmation.

## Platform Posture Cards

The Organizations page surfaces downstream posture summaries so platform admins can assess cross-org health without leaving the lifecycle hub:

- **Budget Control — Platform Posture** (emerald): cross-org budget totals, 30-day spend, active overrides, and breach counts. Drill-through to Budgets, Feature Budgets, Economics, and Platform Settings.
- **FinOps Lifecycle** (emerald): billing period counts (total and active), chargeback rules, and ledger snapshots. Drill-through to Billing Periods, Chargeback, and Ledger (Compliance).
- **Gateway Readiness** (violet): total gateway routes, distinct providers, and guardrail rules. Drill-through to Model Gateway, Guardrails, and Provider Profiles.
- **Governance & Audit** (amber): recent audit events, tool policy count, and alert rule count. Drill-through to Audit Log, Tool Policies, and Alert Rules.
- **Org & Access Lifecycle** (blue): total workspaces, API keys, and users across the platform. Drill-through to Workspaces, API Keys, Users, Onboarding, and Platform Settings.
- **Monitoring & Telemetry** (cyan): alert rules, alert firings, OTLP batch and span ingestion counts. Drill-through to Monitoring, Telemetry, and Alert Rules.
- **Governance & Build Context** (amber): guardrail rules, tool policies, capture policies, eval experiments, agents, and workflow runs. Drill-through to Governance, Evaluation Studio, Agents, and Platform Settings.

### Observe Posture Endpoint

`GET /analytics/platform-admin-observe-posture` (platform admin only) returns:

- **monitoring_context**: alert rules, alert firings (7d), guardrail events (7d)
- **telemetry_context**: OTLP batches (7d), spans ingested (7d)
- **governance_context**: guardrail rules, tool policies, capture policies
- **build_context**: eval experiments, eval datasets, agents, workflow runs (7d)

## Lifecycle Boundaries

Organizations owns the **lifecycle control plane** — create, suspend, reactivate, delete. It does not own:

- **Org Profile**: org-admin self-service rename and profile edits inside one organization.
- **Platform Settings**: compliance, retention, backup, email, and storage configuration.
- **Onboarding**: guided setup flow for workspace, gateway, and safety configuration.

Each posture card links to the owning surface. Organizations summarizes but does not duplicate editing controls.

## Safety Rules

The default organization and the organization you are currently switched into cannot be suspended or deleted. Switch to another organization before deleting a non-default org.

Deleting an organization removes its workspaces and tenant memberships. Users who no longer belong to any organization or workspace are deactivated, while platform admins are preserved.
