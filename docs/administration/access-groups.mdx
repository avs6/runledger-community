---
title: "Access Groups"
description: "Workspace-scoped governance groups for member assignment, budgets, and guardrail envelopes."
---

The **Access Groups** page lets workspace admins define reusable governance bundles for a workspace. Each group can carry a budget envelope, guardrail profile, and dashboard filter context, then map users into that policy surface.

## Who Can Manage Access Groups

- Workspace admins can create, edit, deactivate, and manage group membership.
- Org admins and org managers inherit workspace-admin capability for the active workspace.
- Non-admin members can review the dashboard summary, but they cannot mutate groups or membership.

## What Lives Here

- Create and update access groups.
- Deactivate groups that should no longer grant membership.
- Add and remove members.
- Attach budget and guardrail posture to a reusable workspace group.

## Product Fit

Access Groups are a workspace-scoped governance primitive. They should not become a parallel organization model or a replacement for workspaces. Use them to shape access and policy inside a workspace, not to model legacy teams or projects.
