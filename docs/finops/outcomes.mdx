Cost alone does not tell you whether the spend was worth it. Record a business **outcome** on a run, session, or user and RunLedger joins it to the metered cost so you can see cost-per-success, ROI, and workflow-level business impact.

<Frame caption="Outcomes & ROI - outcome ledger plus cost-per-success, workflow ROI, and success-rate trends.">
  <img src="/images/outcomes.png" alt="Outcomes & ROI" />
</Frame>

## What the page includes

- **Outcome ledger** - create, filter, edit, and delete recorded outcomes
- **Outcome summary** - success rate, cost-per-success, and ROI by outcome type
- **Trend view** - success-rate trend for recent windows
- **Workflow ROI** - cost and value by feature tag and outcome type
- **Quality correlation** - how average score relates to successful business outcomes

## Record an outcome

```python
rl.outcome("refund_resolved", True, run_id=run_id, value_usd=12.00)
```

You can also use the UI form on the Outcomes page or call `POST /outcomes` directly.

## Manage recorded outcomes

RunLedger now supports the full lifecycle for recorded outcomes:

- create
- list and filter
- get by id
- update
- delete

This makes the outcomes page both an analytics surface and the operator ledger behind that analysis.

## Why it matters

Cost-per-success is the headline ROI metric. It lets you compare:

- a cheaper model that fails more often
- a costlier model that succeeds more often

on the only axis that matters: **cost to actually get the job done**.
