---
title: "Billing"
description: "Billing periods, billing summary, exports, and finance-ready reconciliation for RunLedger usage."
---

The Billing area is the finance-facing companion to observability and FinOps. It combines two related views in one place:

- **Billing periods** - managed periods that can be opened, closed, exported, and reconciled
- **Billing summary** - rollup view for billable versus non-billable usage across recent periods

## Billing summary

The summary view inside Billing shows:

- total cost
- billable versus non-billable split
- total calls
- period-by-period bars
- CSV and JSON export

This summary used to be reachable as a separate top-level page, but it now lives inside Billing so finance workflows stay together.

## Billing periods

Use the periods view when you need:

- period lifecycle management
- close-period actions
- export per-period CSV, signed JSON, QuickBooks, or NetSuite payloads
- detailed reconciliation and breakdown per period
